Announcements as the games write them in sound/sentences.txt. Pick one to load it into the text box (word-by-word style).

voxtalk speaks text in the voice of Half-Life's VOX facility announcer.

The voice is generated by AT&T FlexTalk, the 1997 speech engine, running unmodified in your browser: a small emulated PC (v86) boots a tiny kernel that loads the original engine files. The processing measured from the game's own sound/vox files is then applied: slowed playback, the 137 ms echo, filtering, 8-bit output and, for words the game has, its word lengths and pitch.

Text syntax in word-by-word style follows the game's sentences: word(s15 e80) plays 15–80 % of a word, a group on its own like (e90) applies to the following words, p = pitch %, v = volume %, t = time compression %. Commas and full stops become pauses.

A Windows version and the source code are on GitHub.

FlexTalk is AT&T's; its engine files are included unmodified. Half-Life and the VOX announcer are Valve's, and the bundled sound effects are Valve's original Half-Life sounds. Not affiliated with Valve or AT&T. MP3 encoding by lamejs (LGPL). See third-party notices.
